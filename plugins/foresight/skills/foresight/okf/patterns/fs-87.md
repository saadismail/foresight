# FS-87 · Non-standard account persona (store reviewer, shared demo, service) not modelled

Rules and flows are designed for the typical account: one real employee with the company SSO on their own device. One-approved-device-per-user, SSO-first login with credential login as a collapsed secondary link, and per-user session revocation all assume it. A store reviewer, shared demo/QA account or service identity breaks those assumptions: every login on the shared review account revokes the reviewer's device, the team's own smoke tests during review sign the reviewer out, and a reviewer without a company Google account never finds the fallback login. Everything passes for employees; store review rejects the build (Guideline 2.1) or reports 'session expired'.

**Triggers**
- a per-user invariant (one device, one session, one active token) applies to an account type that is deliberately shared (store review, demo, QA, bot)
- about to sign in repeatedly to, or smoke-test with, a shared production account such as the store review account while a review may be in progress
- the app offers SSO plus a fallback credential login and the fallback renders as a de-emphasized or collapsed link
- preparing a store submission that hands the reviewer demo credentials

**Guards**
- At design time list the non-standard account types (store reviewer, demo, QA, service) and give each an explicit exemption flag or isolated policy in the schema
- Write a test that the shared/review account can hold N concurrent approved devices without revoking siblings, next to the single-user test
- Never smoke-test with the live store review account; use a separate QA account under the same policy
- Before every store submission walk the login flow on a fresh device as a user with no SSO account and confirm the fallback path is visible without prior knowledge
