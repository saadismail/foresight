# FS-96 · Extracted document value trusted without a field-format check and an independent control total

OCR, vision or text extraction assigns a value to a field from a low-fidelity read (a downscaled multi-row tile) or from its position on the line (the leading numeric token) rather than from the field's defining format. Visually similar digits (33.00 vs 30.00) and neighbouring numeric fields (a 3-decimal pack size in the retail slot) are substituted silently. Only a reconciliation against a printed control total that happens to cover that field exposes it, and net-only checks miss gross or retail errors.

**Triggers**
- a price, amount or quantity is read off a full-page or multi-row tile instead of a native-resolution crop of that single cell
- a field is taken as 'the leading/Nth numeric token on the line' without checking its format (a currency slot showing 3 decimals)
- the only reconciliation planned is against the net total while the document also prints gross, retail or group subtotals

**Guards**
- Re-read every extracted price/amount from a targeted native-resolution crop of its cell before accepting it
- Validate each field's format before assigning it (currency has exactly 2 decimals; pack-size and quantity patterns differ) and send an explicit unknown (unit_retail: 0 for price-book resolution) instead of a guess
- Reconcile every extracted column against each printed control it feeds: qty x price against gross and group subtotals, retail against the retail-dollar total, not only net
