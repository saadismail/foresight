# FS-32 · Side effects not ordered against the commit or outcome they depend on

Notifications, cache invalidation, external calls, ledger rows and 'added' reports fire before validation/commit or are recorded from intent instead of the confirmed result: cache.delete inside atomic, notify before a later field is validated, a guard appended after side effects, a local record deleted before the remote revoke is confirmed, outbox ops without enforced dependencies, failure counters rolled back with the raise. Success paths look right; rollback and partial-failure paths leak.

**Triggers**
- a multi-step create/transition/share method that emits notifications, webhooks or external I/O
- cache invalidation or a signal handler next to a DB write inside transaction.atomic
- an outbox/queue op that needs another op's result (depends_on) or is enqueued inside a loop
- bookkeeping (ledger, result set, local credential record) written next to a call that can fail or no-op

**Guards**
- Validate all inputs before any side effect, and fire notifications and cache invalidation via transaction.on_commit
- Record outcomes from what the apply call confirmed per item, not from the precomputed diff
- Enforce op dependencies at the single shared dispatch entry point; test dependent + dependency enqueued together and exact enqueue counts
- Test the failure path: counters persist across a raised exception and nothing is notified when validation fails
